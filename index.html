Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this retreat for? ▾

This retreat is open to Arabs of all ages and backgrounds living in North America. Whether you're coming solo, as a couple, or with your whole family, everyone is welcome — from grandparents to little ones.

Can non-Arabs attend? ▾

This retreat is designed as a space for Arabs to connect with their culture and community. We ask that attendees are of Arab heritage or part of an Arab family.

How much does it cost to attend? ▾

Registration is $350 per adult and $150 per child (under 12). Family packages (2 adults + up to 3 children) are available for $750. A $100 deposit is required at registration, with the remaining balance due by June 15th.

What's included in the price? ▾

Your registration covers all meals throughout the week, accommodation in shared cabins, access to all cultural workshops, sports tournaments, cooking competitions, evening social events, and all activity materials.

When and where is the retreat? ▾

The retreat runs July 14–20, 2026 at a scenic campground in Ontario, Canada. Full address and directions will be sent upon registration confirmation.

What is the refund policy? ▾

Cancellations before June 1st are eligible for a full refund minus the $100 deposit. Cancellations after June 1st are non-refundable, but your spot may be transferred to another attendee.

How do I register? ▾

Fill out the registration form on this page and submit your deposit online. You'll receive a confirmation email within 48 hours.
